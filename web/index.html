Getting started

        
The Agentic Designer is where an organisation of AI agents is
          designed, checked and published. You describe the organisation — its
          teams, agents, what they may do and with what data — as a
          model; the designer checks every change against the
          model's rules, and publishing turns the model into running systems.

        
    
          
  1. Say who you are in the as box at the top
            left. A workspace is visible only to its members, so use the same
            name every time.
    2. 
          
  2. Choose or create a workspace with the
            Workspace list and its ＋ ✎
            🗑 buttons.
    3. 
          
  3. Open an organisation: New organisation,
            Load example… (a shipped example), or Import from
            file… (a *.system.yaml from your computer), all on
            the Org chart screen.
    4. 
          
  4. Design it on the Canvas or in the
            Components editors, then Save.
    5. 
          
  5. Publish… when the Issues tab is clear:
            review what the design grants, tick that you have, then request
            deployment.
    6. 
        


        
The model, in five ideas

        
    
          
  • Organisation and teams. Every design has one
            organisation; it is the root team. Teams contain sub-teams and
            agents. An agent belongs to exactly one team.
    • 
          
  • Agents and sub-agents. An agent is a member of the
            organisation, with a team, a mandate and human counterparts. A
            sub-agent is a worker owned by one agent, called like a tool, and
            never wider than its parent.
    • 
          
  • What an agent may do. Capabilities (directly, or
            through roles — its own or its team's), knowledge it consults,
            skills, plugins and tools it holds, data it produces or relies on.
    • 
          
  • Where it runs. Each agent is deployed into one or
            more environments (sandboxes).
    • 
          
  • Rules. Policies, guardrails, separations of
            duties, decisions and mandates bound what anyone may decide.
    • 
        

        
The model is written in UML: every relationship is a composition
          (a part moves with its whole), an association (a reference, drawable
          from either end), a usage, a realisation or a deployment. That
          decides how it is drawn and what dragging does.


        
Workspaces

        
A workspace groups organisations and the people who may see them.

        
    
          
  • ＋ creates one; ✎ renames the open one.
    • 
          
  • 🗑 deletes it. Only its owner may; you type its name to
            confirm, and if it holds organisations you tick that they go too.
            There is no undo.
    • 
          
  • People (or the Workspace screen) adds members
            with a role: viewer, reviewer, editor, admin or owner.
    • 
        


        
Opening an organisation

        
    
          
  • New organisation starts an empty design; its
            name is required.
    • 
          
  • Load example… opens one of the shipped
            organisations, laid out.
    • 
          
  • Import from file… opens a design file from your
            computer into a workspace you choose. A file that is not a design is
            refused with the reason under the file field. From a shell:
            orgagents examples import path/to/x.system.yaml --user you.
    • 
        


        
The canvas

        
The canvas is a picture of the model; every gesture is a model
          operation, and the model answers — accepted, with what else changed,
          or refused, with why. The status line at the top right says which.

Relationships (above the canvas) filters what is
          drawn, by UML kind and, with ▾, by relationship. It hides
          lines, never links, and is remembered in your browser.

          pictures of one model (+ adds one). tree,
          layered and grid arrange the open diagram.

        
Explorer (left, next to Palette) lists everything in
          the model, including what is not drawn.


        
Properties and the Components editors

        
Select a box to edit it in Properties (right). The
          Components menu opens the same form full-size for every kind
          — a list of them, the form, and what refers to each; Agents
          has its own screen.

        
    
          
  • Fields marked * are required.
    • 
          
  • References are searchable pickers: type to filter what the design
            has, choose to add, × to remove. If there is nothing to
            choose, the picker says what to add first.
    • 
          
  • A value the model refuses is put back, with the reason under the
            field.
    • 
        


        
Forms

        
Every form marks what it requires with *.
          On submit, each field that is missing or wrong is outlined and the
          reason is written under it; editing the field clears it.


        
Panels

        
Every side panel can be resized from its inner edge (double-click to
          reset), minimised –, maximised □ and restored
          ❐. Your choices are remembered in this browser.


        
Saving, history, locks

        
    
          
  • Save writes a new version; ↶
            ↷ undo and redo your unsaved steps.
    • 
          
  • History lists versions and restores one.
    • 
          
  • Lock stops others editing while you work; an admin
            can break a stale lock.
    • 
          
  • A draft may be incomplete — a new tool that wraps nothing
            yet — but never broken; publishing requires it complete.
    • 
        


        
Issues

        
The Issues tab and the strip above the canvas list what the
          validator and the model's rules find. Click an issue to select the
          component it is about.


        
Reviewing authority

        
The canvas and forms show what was declared. The authority
          review shows what the design grants, resolved: what
          each agent may actually decide once team inheritance, roles and
          mandates combine; which pairs of decisions are kept apart and whether
          anyone holds both; where each agent's work lives (its placement, a
          unit crossed with an environment); and what each person is
          accountable for. It is read-only — change the design to change it.

        
    
          
  • It is the first step of Publish…: nothing is
            requested until you tick that you have reviewed it.
    • 
          
  • Review authority… on the Org chart opens it any
            time; ← Back to the design returns you.
    • 
          
  • An agent's Effective authority — its resolved
            decisions, each marked declared here or inherited — is shown in its
            Properties and on the Agents screen, as of the last save.
    • 
        


        
Publishing

        
Publish… first asks you to review what the design
          grants (above), then validates and compiles it for a
          target (local, LangGraph, ADK, MAF, Terraform for AWS/Azure/GCP) and
          asks the platform to run it. Compilation is traced: every element and
          link of the model reaches what is built, or is named as not built.
          Request deployment is enabled once the design compiles, you
          may publish, and the review is ticked.


        
Catalog and Marketplace

        
Catalog holds the approved building blocks a design
          may choose from — models, MCP servers, environment templates,
          permission sets, plugins, guardrails. A new entry is
          proposed until approved. Marketplace shares
          agents, skills, plugins, workflows, sandbox templates and sessions
          between organisations.


        
Sessions and Operations

        
Sessions lists runs of published organisations, each
          with its trace. Operations shows alerts and per-agent
          health.


        
Command line

        
orgagents examples list
orgagents examples load northwind --user you
orgagents examples import my.system.yaml --user you
orgagents spec validate my.system.yaml
orgagents metamodel check my.system.yaml     # the model's rules
orgagents metamodel trace my.system.yaml     # model → built artifacts
orgagents compile my.system.yaml --target local


        
When something does not work

        
    
          
  • Nothing to see: check the as box is the
            name you used — a workspace is visible only to its members.
    • 
          
  • A drop or link is refused: the status line says
            which rule refused it and which element breaks it.
    • 
          
  • Cannot edit: you may be a viewer, or someone holds
            the lock (the badge next to your name says).
    • 
          
  • After an update: rebuild with
            docker compose up --build and reload the page.

Who may decide what

        
Effective authority, resolved the way the phase gate
          resolves it. A line reading inherited was never declared on
          the agent — it came down the tree.

        

          
Open an organisation to resolve its authority.

        

      

      

        
Separation of duties

        
A team is a scope and may hold both sides; an agent is
          a principal and may not.

        

        
Where the work lives

        
Placements: an org unit crossed with an environment
          class. Agents inside one share a volume and a process namespace and
          reach each other without a rule; everything not listed as reachable
          is denied.

        

        
People

        
What a person may decide. Nothing here says
          what they may reach: a person signs into the systems under their own
          employer's identity, and a permission this platform cannot enforce
          would be worse than none.
