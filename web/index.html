Who may decide what

Effective authority, resolved the way the phase gate resolves it. A line reading inherited was never declared on the agent — it came down the tree.

Open an organisation to resolve its authority.

Separation of duties

A team is a scope and may hold both sides; an agent is a principal and may not.

Where the work lives

Placements: an org unit crossed with an environment class. Agents inside one share a volume and a process namespace and reach each other without a rule; everything not listed as reachable is denied.

People

What a person may decide. Nothing here says what they may reach: a person signs into the systems under their own employer's identity, and a permission this platform cannot enforce would be worse than none.
