# A real approval matrix, transcribed verbatim

Source: `Exhibit 1 to Financial Policy - Authorization Matrix`, published at
`https://c4-media.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/20123120/20221024_Delegation_of_Authority_Matrix.pdf`
(version `V2022a`, updated 1 January 2022). The prior version, `V2020a`
(approved 9 March 2020), is published at
`https://c4-media.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/07103804/20210111_Copy_of_Delegation_of_Authority_Matrix.pdf`
and has identical rows; it is the cleaner extraction and is the one
transcribed below. A US non-profit — the row *Wire Transfers - Incoming
(PMS/Federal Draws)* names the federal Payment Management System, so it draws
federal funds.

**This document is evidence, not design.** Nothing here was written by us, and
nothing has been tidied. Wording, capitalisation, spacing, the inconsistent
footnote ordering and the threshold gaps are all as published. It exists so
that a test of the mandate model is a test against something we did not
author — the circularity the worked examples cannot escape on their own.

Columns, in the published order:

`Board of Directors (BOD)` · `BOD Chair` · `BOD Finance Committee` ·
`Finance Committee Chair` · `Chief Executive Officer -ED` ·
`Chief Financial Officer` · `Controller` · `Immediate Supervisor`

A `✓` means the column may approve that row. A `-` means it may not.
